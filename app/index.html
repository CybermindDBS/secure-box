Create your master password

It encrypts the key to everything in your vault. It's never stored or sent anywhere — Secure Box can't reset it for you.

Use 12+ characters — a short sentence only you would think of works well. Don't save it in your browser or Google account.

Save your Emergency Kit

If you ever forget your master password, this kit's recovery key is the only way back in.

  • Print it or keep it on a USB stick
  • Never store it in Google Drive or email
  • Make a new one any time from the ⋯ menu
